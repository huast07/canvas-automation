Improved Euler method

Illustrative course: Differential equations · Numerical methods

Why this matters

Uses a predictor and averages two slopes.

Scope: unknown

From first principles

Improved Euler estimates the slope at both the beginning and a predicted end point, then averages them.

Assumptions

Assume the functions and operations used below are defined under the stated conditions. Check the cited source for the full hypotheses.

Reasoning / derivation

Let k₁ = f(tₙ,yₙ), predict y* = yₙ + hk₁, and take k₂ = f(tₙ+h,y*). Averaging slopes gives yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + h(k₁+k₂)/2.

Example

For y′ = t + y, y(0) = 1 and h = 0.5: k₁ = 1, y* = 1.5, k₂ = 2, so the improved estimate is 1.75.

PROBLEM TYPE

Compare numerical methods

Use the average of the starting and predicted ending slopes.

Reasoning / derivation

The predictor uses k₁, and the correction averages k₁ and k₂.

Worked example

For y′=t+y, y(0)=1 and h=0.5, compute one improved Euler step.

  1. k₁=1 and the Euler predictor is 1.5.
  2. k₂=0.5+1.5=2.
  3. y₁=1+0.5(1+2)/2=1.75.
