Box probabilities and uncertainty checks

PHY293 · Waves and Modern Physics · Quantum dynamics

Why this matters

Calculate spatial moments and use kinetic energy to obtain momentum spread.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Level 1 · A box eigenstate has a nonuniform probability density even though its mean position lies at the center. A stationary density does not imply zero kinetic energy.

Assumptions

Use a normalized eigenstate on [0,L]. For the course’s box calculation, symmetry gives mean momentum zero and ⟨p2⟩=2mEn. These statements should not be applied blindly to arbitrary travelling states.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · ⟨x⟩=L/2,⟨x2⟩=L2(13−12n2π2),σp=nπℏL. Thus σxσp=ℏ2n2π23−2≥ℏ/2. Interval probabilities follow by integrating (2/L)sin2(nπx/L).

Example

Level 3 · For n=1, the uncertainty product is about 0.568ℏ. For the ground state, probability between L/4 and 3L/4 is 1/2+1/π≃0.818. Probability between −L/4 and 3L/4 is instead 3/4+1/(2π)≃0.909, because the negative-x segment is outside the well.
Common trap: PS8 Q5(b) prints the negative lower limit, but its solution uses the positive lower limit. The two answers address different intervals.

PROBLEM TYPE

8. Box energy levels and spatial probabilities

Level 1 · A box eigenstate has a nonuniform probability density even though its mean position lies at the center. A stationary density does not imply zero kinetic energy.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · ⟨x⟩=L/2,⟨x2⟩=L2(13−12n2π2),σp=nπℏL. Thus σxσp=ℏ2n2π23−2≥ℏ/2. Interval probabilities follow by integrating (2/L)sin2(nπx/L).

Worked example

For the ground state on [0,L], calculate the probability asked in PS8 Q5(b), from −L/4 to 3L/4.

  1. Normalize the sine: ψ=2/Lsin(πx/L) inside, zero outside.
  2. The requested interval overlaps the well on [0,3L/4]. Use sin2u=(1−cos2u)/2.
  3. P=[xL−sin(2πx/L)2π]03L/4=34+12π≃0.909.
  4. The posted solution instead uses [L/4,3L/4] and obtains about 0.818. Both integrations are correct for their respective limits; the printed limits differ.
