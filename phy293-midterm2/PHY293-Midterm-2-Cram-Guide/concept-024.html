Lifetime, linewidth and energy uncertainty

PHY293 · Waves and Modern Physics · Wave packets

Why this matters

Relate finite temporal duration to spectral spread without confusing the time scale with an exact period.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Level 1 · A long-lived state has a narrow energy distribution; a short-lived state has a broad one. The course uses a time–energy uncertainty estimate for lifetimes and characteristic change times.

Assumptions

For estimates use ΔEΔt≳ℏ/2, stating the convention. A spectral full width, half width and standard deviation are different; time is not a position-like operator here.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · ΔE~ℏ2Δt. For a small photon wavelength spread, differentiate E=hc/λ to get |ΔE|/E≃|Δλ|/λ. A coherent two-level oscillation instead has exact period 2πℏ/|Eb−Ea|.

Example

Level 3 · For PS8 Q3’s 1 ms lifetime, the minimum energy-spread estimate is 3.29×10−13eV. At 550 nm, fractional wavelength spread is about 1.46×10−13. Different rounding yields the posted 1.4×10⁻¹³.
Common trap: An uncertainty-based characteristic time is not automatically the exact recurrence time.

6. Uncertainty, linewidth and confinement estimates

Level 1 · A long-lived state has a narrow energy distribution; a short-lived state has a broad one. The course uses a time–energy uncertainty estimate for lifetimes and characteristic change times.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · ΔE~ℏ2Δt. For a small photon wavelength spread, differentiate E=hc/λ to get |ΔE|/E≃|Δλ|/λ. A coherent two-level oscillation instead has exact period 2πℏ/|Eb−Ea|.

Worked example

PS8 Q3: a state has lifetime 1 ms and emits around 550 nm. Estimate energy spread and fractional wavelength spread.

  1. Using the course estimate ΔE≃ℏ/(2τ), obtain ΔE≃3.29×10−13eV.
  2. The photon energy is 1240/550≃2.25eV.
  3. For a narrow line, Δλ/λ≃ΔE/E≃1.46×10−13. This is a linewidth estimate, not an exact oscillation period.

9. Superposition normalization and energy moments

Level 1 · A long-lived state has a narrow energy distribution; a short-lived state has a broad one. The course uses a time–energy uncertainty estimate for lifetimes and characteristic change times.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · ΔE~ℏ2Δt. For a small photon wavelength spread, differentiate E=hc/λ to get |ΔE|/E≃|Δλ|/λ. A coherent two-level oscillation instead has exact period 2πℏ/|Eb−Ea|.

Worked example

2025 QII: Ψ=A(ψ2+12ψ3), where the basis states are normalized. Find A, energy moments and energy uncertainty.

  1. 1=A2(1+1/4), so A=2/5. Probabilities are 4/5 and 1/5.
  2. E2=4E1, E3=9E1. Thus ⟨E⟩=(4/5)4E1+(1/5)9E1=5E1.
  3. ⟨E2⟩=(4/5)16E12+(1/5)81E12=29E12, so σE=29−25E1=2E1.
  4. The course uncertainty estimate gives Δt~ℏ/(4E1). The actual density beat period is 2πℏ/(5E1), a different quantity.
