Runge–Kutta method

Illustrative course: Differential equations · Numerical methods

Why this matters

Combines several estimated slopes per step.

Scope: unknown

From first principles

A four-stage Runge–Kutta method combines several slope estimates across a step.

Assumptions

Assume the functions and operations used below are defined under the stated conditions. Check the cited source for the full hypotheses.

Reasoning / derivation

The classical method uses slopes k1,k2,k3,k4 with weights 1,2,2,1: yn+1=yn+h6(k1+2k2+2k3+k4). Its error-order proof requires additional assumptions and a Taylor-series argument.

Example

If y′=1, every stage slope is 1, so the method advances y by exactly h.
