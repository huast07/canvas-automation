Choose the heat-transfer modes

CHE260 · Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer · Foundations

Unofficial study example · Fall 2026. This independently written guide is based on the posted CHE260 Quiz 2 scope. It is not an instructor-approved answer key. Quercus source links require course access. Worked examples are original unless identified otherwise.

Why this matters

Identify which physical mechanisms act in each region and at each boundary.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Conduction transfers energy through microscopic interactions; convection exchanges heat between a surface and moving fluid; radiation transfers electromagnetic energy and can cross a vacuum.

Assumptions

Choose modes for each region and boundary. An opaque solid interior is normally modelled by conduction. A gas gap may have conduction, circulation and radiation.

Reasoning / derivation

Trace the physical path. Through a wall: conduction. At a wall exposed to air: convection, often radiation as a second path. Across an evacuated gap: radiation remains. A convection coefficient already includes the near-wall conductive transport in its fluid model; do not automatically add a second gas-conduction path for the same process.

Example

Original example: a hot solid plate in a vacuum still loses energy by radiation. Removing air removes convection, not radiation.
