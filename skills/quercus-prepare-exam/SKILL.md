---
name: quercus-prepare-exam
description: Guide a student through course sync, exam scope, practice and exam preparation in one workflow.
---
Ask which course and exam the student is preparing for, the exam date and timezone, and how much time is available. For any exam-preparation request, even when the student does not explicitly ask for scope, identify and check the selected exam’s scope before choosing a study plan or practice. Use quercus-exam-scope when the scope is unknown, incomplete, stale or for a different exam; reuse existing results only after checking their exam identity, sources and freshness. Locate the Quercus project and existing study-data before concluding that tools or materials are unavailable. Guide them through quercus-courses and quercus-lecture-notes as needed. Offer quercus-document-import when notes, scope or past exams are missing. For an imminent exam use quercus-cram; otherwise suggest quercus-study-planner, quercus-exam-prep and quercus-mock-exam across multiple sessions. Explain each step, report missing evidence, and let the student choose how to spend their time. Never request their token in chat or transmit their course materials to an external service without consent.
