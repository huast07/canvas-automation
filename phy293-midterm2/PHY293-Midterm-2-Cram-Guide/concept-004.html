Normalization: direct and piecewise

PHY293 · Waves and Modern Physics · Foundations

Why this matters

Find the scale factor that makes total probability one.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Level 1 · An arbitrary shape f(x) does not yet specify a normalized state. Write Ψ=Af. Normalization determines the magnitude of A, not its physically irrelevant global phase.

Assumptions

Assume f is square-integrable and nonzero. If A is stated to be real, choose the positive root unless a sign is otherwise specified.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · 1=|A|2∫|f|2dx⇒|A|=(∫|f|2dx)−1/2. Split the integral at every change of formula. For the triangular PS7 shape with peak at a and endpoint b, the two shape integrals are a/3 and (b−a)/3, so the total is b/3.

Example

Level 3 · PS7 Q6 gives A=3/b and P(x<a)=A2a/3=a/b. For b=2a, symmetry gives 1/2, agreeing with the integral. Do not treat the area under the triangular amplitude as the probability.

PROBLEM TYPE

5. Normalize a shape and calculate probabilities

Level 1 · An arbitrary shape f(x) does not yet specify a normalized state. Write Ψ=Af. Normalization determines the magnitude of A, not its physically irrelevant global phase.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · 1=|A|2∫|f|2dx⇒|A|=(∫|f|2dx)−1/2. Split the integral at every change of formula. For the triangular PS7 shape with peak at a and endpoint b, the two shape integrals are a/3 and (b−a)/3, so the total is b/3.

Worked example

PS8 Q4: normalize ψ=Ax(L−x) on 0≤x≤L, then find left-half probability and mean position.

  1. ∫0Lx2(L−x)2dx=L5/30, so A=30/L5/2.
  2. The density is symmetric about L/2. Equal halves give P(0≤x≤L/2)=1/2.
  3. The same symmetry gives ⟨x⟩=L/2. This shape is admissible as a state but is not a single box energy eigenfunction.
