Combined convection and radiation

CHE260 · Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer · Surface heat exchange

Unofficial study example · Fall 2026. This independently written guide is based on the posted CHE260 Quiz 2 scope. It is not an instructor-approved answer key. Quercus source links require course access. Worked examples are original unless identified otherwise.

Why this matters

Add two surface heat-loss paths without double-counting or assuming equal sinks.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

A surface can exchange heat with air and surrounding walls simultaneously. Their heat rates add because they are separate paths from the surface.

Assumptions

Only combine coefficients into one temperature difference when bulk-fluid and radiative-surroundings temperatures are equal. Do not add radiation again if a provided h is already combined.

Reasoning / derivation

Factor Ts4−Tsurr4=(Ts−Tsurr)(Ts+Tsurr)(Ts2+Tsurr2). Thus hrad=εσ(Ts+Tsurr)(Ts2+Tsurr2). If T∞=Tsurr, Q˙=(h+hrad)A(Ts−T∞). This factorization is exact for the model, but h_rad depends on unknown T_s; solve or iterate consistently.

Example

Original example: use the 350 K surface and 300 K surroundings above, with h=10 and area 1 m². Convection is 500 W; radiation is 313.268 W; total is 813.268 W. Here hrad=6.26535 W/(m2K).

PROBLEM TYPE

6 · Surface balance with radiation

A surface can exchange heat with air and surrounding walls simultaneously. Their heat rates add because they are separate paths from the surface.

Reasoning / derivation

Factor Ts4−Tsurr4=(Ts−Tsurr)(Ts+Tsurr)(Ts2+Tsurr2). Thus hrad=εσ(Ts+Tsurr)(Ts2+Tsurr2). If T∞=Tsurr, Q˙=(h+hrad)A(Ts−T∞). This factorization is exact for the model, but h_rad depends on unknown T_s; solve or iterate consistently.

Worked example

Original example: a 1 m² surface at 350 K with emissivity 0.8 loses heat to 300 K air and walls. h=10 W/(m² K). What electrical power maintains it?

  1. Q˙conv=10(1)(350−300)=500 W.
  2. Q˙rad=0.8(5.67×10−8)(1)(3504−3004)=313.268 W.
  3. Energy balance gives P=813.268 W. Alternatively hrad=313.268/50=6.26535, so combined h is 16.26535 W/(m² K).
  4. For a prescribed power and unknown Ts, solve P=hA(Ts−T∞)+εσA(Ts4−Tsurr4); do not evaluate h_rad at an inconsistent guessed temperature.
