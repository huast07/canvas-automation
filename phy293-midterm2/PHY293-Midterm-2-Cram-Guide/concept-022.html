Position–momentum uncertainty and confinement

PHY293 · Waves and Modern Physics · Wave packets

Why this matters

Convert localization into a lower bound or scale for momentum spread.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Level 1 · Position and wavenumber are Fourier partners. A sharply localized state cannot have a sharply defined momentum. This is a property of the state, not merely a bad measuring instrument.

Assumptions

For standard deviations the precise bound is σxσp≥ℏ/2. Size estimates such as Δx~a and Δp~ℏ/a give order-of-magnitude energies, not exact eigenvalues.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · Use p=ℏk to convert σxσk≥1/2. If ⟨p⟩=0, ⟨K⟩=σp2/(2m). Thus fixing σx gives ⟨K⟩≥ℏ2/(8mσx2). With a confinement length a, the energy scale is ℏ2/(ma2).

Example

Level 3 · For a stated σx=1.00 nm, σp≥5.27×10−26kgm/s, corresponding to electron velocity spread at least 5.79×104m/s. This is not a nonzero minimum velocity for every measurement.
Common trap: Do not replace mean momentum by momentum uncertainty or silently change a factor-of-two convention.

PROBLEM TYPE

6. Uncertainty, linewidth and confinement estimates

Level 1 · Position and wavenumber are Fourier partners. A sharply localized state cannot have a sharply defined momentum. This is a property of the state, not merely a bad measuring instrument.

Reasoning / derivation

Level 2 · Use p=ℏk to convert σxσk≥1/2. If ⟨p⟩=0, ⟨K⟩=σp2/(2m). Thus fixing σx gives ⟨K⟩≥ℏ2/(8mσx2). With a confinement length a, the energy scale is ℏ2/(ma2).

Worked example

PS8 Q3: a state has lifetime 1 ms and emits around 550 nm. Estimate energy spread and fractional wavelength spread.

  1. Using the course estimate ΔE≃ℏ/(2τ), obtain ΔE≃3.29×10−13eV.
  2. The photon energy is 1240/550≃2.25eV.
  3. For a narrow line, Δλ/λ≃ΔE/E≃1.46×10−13. This is a linewidth estimate, not an exact oscillation period.
