---
name: quercus-prepare-exam
description: Guide a student through course sync, exam scope, practice and exam preparation in one workflow.
---
Ask which course and exam the student is preparing for, the exam date and timezone, and how much time is available. For any exam-preparation request, even when the student does not explicitly ask for scope, identify and check the selected exam’s scope before choosing a study plan or practice. Use quercus-exam-scope when the scope is unknown, incomplete, stale or for a different exam; reuse existing results only after checking their exam identity, sources and freshness. Locate the Quercus project and existing study-data before concluding that tools or materials are unavailable. Use quercus-courses to identify the course. If lecture sync is missing, invoke and perform quercus-lecture-notes yourself; then invoke and perform quercus-exam-scope yourself if a current reviewed scope is missing. Before invoking cram or exam prep, offer the student a chance to upload or provide local paths to extra materials such as textbooks, filled-in lecture notes, exam guidance or past papers, and wait for those materials or an explicit decision to continue without them. Do not repeat the question if they have already been offered the opportunity and responded, or clearly said there is nothing more to add; a request for a required missing file alone does not replace this offer. Use quercus-document-import and re-index supplied files, then re-check scope if needed. If prerequisites cannot be completed, explain the blocker and pause. For an imminent exam use quercus-cram; otherwise suggest quercus-study-planner, quercus-exam-prep and quercus-mock-exam across multiple sessions. Explain each step, report missing evidence, and let the student choose how to spend their time. Never request their token in chat or transmit their course materials to an external service without consent.
