Heat, heat rate and heat flux

CHE260 · Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer · Foundations

Unofficial study example · Fall 2026. This independently written guide is based on the posted CHE260 Quiz 2 scope. It is not an instructor-approved answer key. Quercus source links require course access. Worked examples are original unless identified otherwise.

Why this matters

Distinguish total energy, power and power per unit area.

Scope: confirmed

From first principles

Heat Q is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. Heat rate Q˙ measures joules transferred each second; flux q′′ measures rate per unit area.

Assumptions

Use the area actually crossed by heat. A uniform flux is needed to replace an area integral by a product.

Reasoning / derivation

Q˙=dQdt,Q˙=∫Aq′′dA. For uniform flux, q′′=Q˙/A. For constant heat rate, Q=Q˙t. Units are J, W=J/s, and W/m².

Example

Original example: 100 W crosses 2 m² for 60 s. Flux is 50 W/m2, while transferred energy is 6000 J.
