Separation of variables

Illustrative course: Differential equations · First-order equations

Why this matters

Rearranges a separable equation so each variable can be integrated.

Scope: unknown

From first principles

A separable equation has the form y′ = g(t)h(y), so its time and state factors can be treated separately where h(y) is nonzero.

Assumptions

Assume the functions and operations used below are defined under the stated conditions. Check the cited source for the full hypotheses.

Reasoning / derivation

Because y′ = dy/dt, dividing by h(y) and integrating gives ∫dy/h(y) = ∫g(t)dt + C on an interval where division is valid. Check any excluded constant solutions separately.

Example

For y′ = 2y and y(0) = 1, separation gives ln|y| = 2t + C; the initial condition selects y = e^(2t).
