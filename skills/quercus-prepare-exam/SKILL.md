---
name: quercus-prepare-exam
description: Guide a student through course sync, exam scope, practice and exam preparation in one workflow.
---
Ask which course and exam the student is preparing for, the exam date and timezone, and how much time is available. Guide them through quercus-courses, quercus-lecture-notes and quercus-exam-scope, reusing existing results only after checking freshness. Offer quercus-document-import when notes, scope or past exams are missing. For an imminent exam use quercus-cram; otherwise suggest quercus-study-planner, quercus-exam-prep and quercus-mock-exam across multiple sessions. Explain each step, report missing evidence, and let the student choose how to spend their time. Never request their token in chat or transmit their course materials to an external service without consent.
