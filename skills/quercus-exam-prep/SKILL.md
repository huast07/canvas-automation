---
name: quercus-exam-prep
description: Categorize past exam problems and build progressive practice from course materials and exam scope.
---
Identify the course materials, past exams, and the stated midterm or final scope. Ask for missing past exams or scope; do not assume lecture-note downloads contain exams. Read available material and identify coverage gaps. Map each past problem to one or more topics with source, question number, and rationale; distinguish uncertain classifications. Build a progression from prerequisite concepts and basic practice to multi-step exam-level problems, aligned to the stated scope. Write original practice questions with hints and worked solutions, and identify any adapted past problems and their source. Invite the learner to attempt questions before revealing solutions when interactive. Do not invent exam topics or claim comprehensive coverage of unreadable files.
